# Master Your Logic Building — Complete 330-Question Learning Path
*(Difficulty re-calibrated for a TRUE BEGINNER — someone who has never coded before, not an experienced programmer.)*

**Key change from the first pass:** things a pro shrugs off — recursion, nested loops, multi-step string/array logic — are genuinely hard the first time you meet them. So recursion is never tagged Easy here, and anything needing two loops or several combined ideas is at least Medium.
